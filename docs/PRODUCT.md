# Ready product brief

Ready prepares people for the dependencies across their day. Its central output is a brief of what must be done, worn, eaten, or brought before departure—not a full routine or another task list.

The Phase 1 demo follows Maya from Design Studio through a Portfolio Review, Pilates, Dinner, and Home. Weather, physical-object tasks, enriched event types, closet suitability, and lower-than-baseline sleep affect the plan. Ready does not infer travel or return-home windows until it has enough timing and location context. Sleep reduces optional recommendation load without changing essentials or making health claims.

Core surfaces are onboarding, Today / Ready Brief, recommendation reasoning, Outfit Journey, Journey Map, Ready Check, Prepare Tomorrow, Closet, and Evening Debrief. User corrections always win. The Pilates cancellation demo proves that a changed source recalculates dependent recommendations and transitions.

Phase 2 can add Google Calendar and Todoist OAuth, a weather provider, richer closet intake, and AI-assisted classification after the deterministic product loop is validated.
